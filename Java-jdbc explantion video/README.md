### 📌 Demonstration Highlights

1. **Database Connectivity:** Live MySQL connection establishment over JDBC driver.
2. **Role-Based Authentication:** BCrypt encrypted login for Admin, Faculty, and Student personas.
3. **Curriculum & Enrollment:** Course allocation, credit weights, and student record management.
4. **Assessment & Grading:** Continuous Internal Assessments (CIA 1 & 2), Semester End Examination (ESE), letter grades (`O` through `RA`), and credit-weighted SGPA calculation per KEC R2024 regulations.
5. **Attendance & Condonation Workflow:** Automatic eligibility enforcement ($\ge$75% eligible, 65%–74.9% medical condonation, $<$65% detained).
6. **Student Activity Points (SAP):** Certification uploads, bonus points, and faculty validation.
7. **Institutional PDF Marksheet:** Live PDF generation featuring college crest, tabular marks, and security verification.
